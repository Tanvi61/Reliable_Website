TECHNICAL FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Specific queries regarding our DGPS / GNSS Control methodology and deliverables.

What is the difference between standard GPS and DGPS?
Standard handheld GPS has an error margin of 3 to 10 meters. DGPS utilizes dual-frequency base-and-rover carrier-phase differential corrections to reduce positional errors down to millimeters.
Why are DGPS control points needed for linear projects like highways?
Linear projects spanning tens or hundreds of kilometers accumulate significant angular error if surveyed only with optical total stations. DGPS provides absolute global reference nodes every 2-5 km to eliminate cumulative error.
Can you link our project to the Survey of India GTS benchmark?
Yes. Our geodetic engineers execute static GNSS ties to the nearest SOI GTS benchmark to establish certified MSL elevations and national grid coordinates.
