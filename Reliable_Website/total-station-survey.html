Total Station Survey

Sub-second electronic theodolite measurement, boundary demarcation, and construction staking across Pune & Maharashtra.

OPTICAL PRECISION & STRUCTURAL POSITIONING

High-Precision Robotic Total Station Surveying & Column Staking

A Total Station Survey remains the gold standard for high-precision civil engineering, structural column staking, and boundary demarcations. Combining electronic theodolites with electronic distance measurement (EDM) and advanced onboard computing, total stations provide sub-second angular resolution and millimeter-accurate distance measurement.

At Reliable Land Survey Consultancy, our field engineering teams deploy 1-second and 0.5-second robotic total stations (Leica / Topcon / Trimble). We provide critical dimensional control for high-rise buildings, industrial plant foundations, railway viaducts, bridge pier alignments, and complex property boundaries across Maharashtra.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Total Station Survey FAQs

Clear answers to common questions about total station surveying, column staking, and angular accuracy.

What is the accuracy of your Total Station surveys? +
Our high-grade 1-second and 0.5-second total stations achieve distance measurement precision of ±1mm + 1.5ppm, providing unmatched millimeter accuracy for structural works.
Can you stake out column centers directly from our AutoCAD file? +
Yes. We load your approved structural .DWG CAD drawings directly into our electronic data collectors to stake column centers, footings, and grid intersections directly on site.
Do you provide boundary dispute resolution surveys? +
Yes. We correlate official revenue records (Gut maps/TILR) with on-site physical measurements to establish undisputed boundary demarcations.
What is the maximum working range and reflectorless capability of your instruments? +
Our robotic and electronic total stations feature reflectorless EDM ranges up to 1,000 meters, allowing accurate measurement of inaccessible points such as overhead high-tension lines, bridge soffits, and building facades.
How do you establish closed traverse control to eliminate cumulative errors? +
We execute rigorous closed loop polygon traverses with angular and linear closure tolerances meeting 1:20,000 or better, with mathematical least-squares adjustment before picking up internal physical features.
Can total stations perform real-time structural monitoring for deflections? +
Yes. We install fixed monitoring prisms on retaining walls, deep excavation pits, and structural columns to detect sub-millimeter settlement or lateral movement over defined construction monitoring intervals.
